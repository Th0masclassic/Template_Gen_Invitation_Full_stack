# Etsy Listing and Ads Plan

Research date: 2 August 2026  
Product: custom digital wedding invitation system  
Primary market: Etsy wedding invitations, wedding websites, digital RSVP, and editable wedding templates

## Executive recommendation

Position this product as a coordinated, made-to-order wedding experience rather than as another inexpensive Canva template. The clearest differentiator is that one design connects the full-screen envelope, invitation PDF, responsive wedding website, editable Canva template, and a private RSVP dashboard scoped to the couple's wedding.

Recommended customer promise:

> A memorable invitation for your guests and one organized place for every reply.

The product should not be advertised until the RSVP form-to-admin flow has passed an end-to-end production test using at least two different wedding codes. The test must prove that each administrator sees only the registrations belonging to that wedding.

## Evidence and current market positioning

### Official Etsy guidance

- Etsy's April 2026 guidance favors short, clear, buyer-friendly titles. Etsy suggests fewer than 15 words, putting the item and its key objective traits first, avoiding repeated words, and moving subjective or promotional language out of the title. Etsy now evaluates the whole listing, including the title, tags, attributes, description, first image, and reviews. [New Guidance for Listing Titles](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- Sellers should use all 13 tags. Each tag can contain no more than 20 characters, including spaces. Etsy recommends diversified, multi-word, long-tail phrases and advises against repeating tags, categories, attributes, intentional misspellings, or unnecessary plurals. The opening sentences of the description should use important keywords naturally. [Keywords 101](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/keywords-101-everything-you-need-to-know/382774281517)
- The first listing image should clearly show the finished item, be bright and high-resolution, and avoid a confusing collage. Etsy recommends at least 2,000 pixels on the shortest side. Current listings support up to 20 photos and two videos of 5-15 seconds. [Creating Listings That Convert](https://www.etsy.com/ca/seller-handbook/article/create-listings-that-convert/366469719354) and [How to Create a Listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing)
- New shops must wait 15 days before starting Etsy Ads. The minimum daily budget is $1, all sellers initially have a maximum daily budget of $25, and Etsy automatically controls the auction bid. Etsy advises allowing roughly 30 days before judging a strategy. [How to Set Up and Manage an Etsy Ads Campaign](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033701174-How-to-Set-Up-and-Manage-an-Etsy-Ads-Campaign)
- Etsy reports ad views, clicks, click rate, cost per click, attributed orders, revenue, search terms, and ROAS. An order may be attributed for 30 days after an ad click, including an order for a different item in the shop. [How to Review Etsy Ads Performance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360034223613-How-to-Review-the-Performance-of-Your-Etsy-Ads)
- Offsite Ads charge 15% on attributed orders while a shop remains below the $10,000 USD threshold in any consecutive 365-day period and participation remains optional. At or above that threshold, participation becomes mandatory and the rate is 12%. [How Etsy's Offsite Ads Work](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work)
- Etsy distinguishes instant downloads from made-to-order digital items. A customized pack prepared after purchase should be listed as made-to-order digital. Digital listings can contain up to five files of 20 MB each and do not support ordinary variations. [How to Manage Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)
- Etsy's May 2026 wedding guidance emphasizes trustworthy information, clear customization, multiple price points, digital formats, and visual storytelling. It also identifies botanical weddings and gothic romance as current style opportunities. [How to Sell to Wedding Shoppers on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/24869259496)

### Positioning inference

The observed DIY website-template market broadly spans about $15-$33, while genuine done-for-you customization can support roughly $50-$79. Low-price listings below this range are common, but competing primarily on price would obscure this product's stronger value: the coordinated outputs and wedding-specific RSVP administration.

Recommended positioning:

- Main offer: premium made-to-order full pack.
- Supporting offer, if operationally desirable: lower-priced self-edit pack without custom preparation.
- Future style-specific listings: ivory/minimalist, botanical/sage, and dark editorial or gothic. Each listing should be materially distinct, not an SEO duplicate.

## Pricing inference

### Recommended price architecture

- Full made-to-order pack: **$59-$79**.
- Self-edit pack, if offered separately: **$29-$39**.
- Recommended initial full-pack price: **$59** while the shop builds verified reviews, provided this price covers customization time, Etsy fees, support, and advertising.
- A strike-through regular price of $79 should be used only if $79 is the genuine price the product will normally carry after the introductory period. Otherwise list at $59 without an artificial discount.

### Profitability inputs to record before advertising

For each order, calculate:

1. sale price after discounts;
2. Etsy transaction, payment-processing, listing, currency, and tax-related fees;
3. average labor cost for design preparation, revisions, and support;
4. hosting, software, and third-party service cost per wedding;
5. expected refund, cancellation, or remediation allowance;
6. contribution margin before ads.

Do not set the ad budget from revenue alone. Set it from contribution margin.

## 45-day Etsy Ads launch plan

### Budget recommendation

- Standard test: **$10/day from day 16 through day 45**, for a maximum 30-day spend of **$300**.
- Lean test when margins or cash flow are tighter: **$5/day**, maximum **$150** over the same period.
- Do not begin at $25/day merely to unlock additional listing strategies. Increase toward that level only after a listing demonstrates profitable conversion.
- If only one listing is ready, advertise the hero listing. If three to five materially different style listings are ready, advertise that entire product family during the learning period.

### Days 1-7: production and listing preflight

- Test at least two wedding codes end to end: guest submission, database write, wedding lookup, administrator login, filtering, and display.
- Confirm that a wedding administrator cannot see another wedding's registrations.
- Test the envelope at Etsy PDF preview dimensions, common mobile viewports, desktop, and print or download output where supported.
- Replace unsupported mockups with real product screens.
- Complete the title, 13 tags, category, attributes, description, gallery, FAQ, shop policies, and made-to-order classification.
- Calculate the contribution margin and break-even ROAS before spending.

### Days 8-15: organic baseline and conversion preparation

- Publish the optimized listing and allow Etsy's indexing to settle.
- Use Etsy Marketplace Insights to compare current demand and competition for the proposed phrases.
- Record organic impressions, visits, favorites, cart adds, demo visits, questions, and sales.
- Test the thumbnail in Etsy's available square, portrait, and landscape previews.
- Prepare quick replies for turnaround, Canva access, RSVP privacy, editing scope, and delivery questions.
- Do not use self-clicks, coordinated favorites, fabricated purchases, or other activity intended to manipulate ranking.

### Days 16-22: begin the paid learning period

- Start Etsy Ads at the selected $5 or $10 daily budget.
- Leave all eligible product-family listings active if the budget can expose each one; otherwise prioritize the main full-pack listing.
- Confirm that ad search terms are relevant to digital wedding invitations, wedding websites, RSVP, and editing intent.
- Make only urgent corrections for misleading or irrelevant matching. Avoid changing the title, thumbnail, price, and tags at the same time.

### Days 23-30: diagnose the funnel

- High impressions with weak click response: improve the first image, title clarity, or visible value proposition.
- Clicks without meaningful engagement: improve the first three gallery images and put the live demo near the start of the description.
- Favorites or carts without purchases: clarify exactly what is included, turnaround, revisions, editing requirements, and digital-only delivery.
- Relevant clicks without orders: review pricing, demo quality, product reliability proof, FAQs, and buyer trust signals.
- Irrelevant search traffic: revise the listing language that is causing the mismatch, then document the change date.

### Days 31-44: hold a stable measurement window

- Preserve a stable title, thumbnail, price, tags, and offer unless a serious defect is discovered.
- Review CTR, CPC, conversion rate, spend, attributed orders, revenue, search terms, and ROAS by listing.
- Separate organic orders from ad-attributed orders when evaluating the campaign.
- Track support time and revision effort so contribution-margin assumptions become more accurate.
- Do not judge a listing solely by one day's activity; Etsy recommends a longer view because attribution can occur for 30 days after a click.

### Day 45: make the keep, improve, or pause decision

For each advertised listing:

- **Scale:** The listing is above its profitability target, search terms are relevant, and the real delivery workload remains sustainable. Increase the daily budget by 20-25% and reassess after seven days.
- **Keep stable:** The listing is near target and conversion evidence is promising but attribution is still developing. Hold the current budget for another 14 days.
- **Improve:** CTR is healthy but conversion is weak. Fix the demo, offer clarity, proof, pricing, FAQ, or product experience before buying more traffic.
- **Pause:** Spend has reached the maximum acceptable acquisition cost without a sale, search terms remain irrelevant, or the listing is below break-even after a meaningful 30-day test.

### Break-even ROAS and decision formulas

Use contribution margin before advertising, not gross revenue:

```text
Contribution margin rate = contribution profit before ads / order revenue
Break-even ROAS = 1 / contribution margin rate
Maximum ad cost per order = contribution profit before ads
Actual ROAS = ad-attributed revenue / ad spend
```

Examples:

| Contribution margin before ads | Break-even ROAS |
| ---: | ---: |
| 40% | 2.50 |
| 50% | 2.00 |
| 60% | 1.67 |
| 70% | 1.43 |

Set a target above break-even to preserve profit. Etsy mentions 2.8 as a broad advertising benchmark, but the correct target is the one supported by this product's own margin and workload. [Etsy's listing promotion guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/648734082834)

### Offsite Ads decision

- Below the $10,000 threshold, keep Offsite Ads enabled only if a 15% attributed-order fee still leaves an acceptable contribution margin.
- At or above the threshold, include the mandatory 12% rate in pricing and profitability calculations.
- Review the Offsite Ads dashboard separately because it does not share the Etsy Ads daily budget or listing controls.
- A premium full pack is better suited to Offsite Ads economics than a heavily discounted DIY template.

## Compliance and trust checklist

### Unsupported sales and testimonial warning

**Do not publish the supplied graphic claiming `2K+ orders`, `2,000+ invitations sold`, named testimonials, or star ratings unless every claim is supported by the actual Etsy shop records and genuine customer reviews.** These claims are not established by the files supplied for this project. Fabricated social proof would mislead buyers and conflict with Etsy's requirement for accurate representation.

Until genuine evidence exists, replace that graphic with one of the following:

- verified product functionality;
- a real, anonymized RSVP workflow;
- a feature comparison;
- a clear customization example;
- the ordering process;
- a live-demo invitation.

Additional launch checks:

- Use real screenshots of the production product wherever functionality is claimed.
- Do not label the item `Bestseller` unless Etsy applies that badge.
- Do not describe a sale as limited-time unless the regular price and end date are genuine.
- Classify custom preparation accurately as made-to-order digital.
- State what the customer receives, what remains editable, and what software is required.
- Confirm licensing for fonts, photographs, illustrations, music, and Canva assets.
- If the item itself was created using AI, add the disclosure required by Etsy's current Seller Policy.
- Keep all payments on Etsy; an external demo must not redirect buyers to an off-Etsy checkout.
- Protect guest data and avoid displaying real names, emails, telephone numbers, dietary information, or access codes in listing media.
- Use only verified customer language in future testimonial images, with permission where required.
